## Copy rules

- Use short, natural Persian sentences. Preserve public account names and English brand names when needed.
- Keep the page focused on introduction and contact. Do not add inactive shopping, login or newsletter controls.
- Do not invent counts, testimonials, awards, medical credentials, certification marks or institutional history.
- Do not state that a pictured food is gluten-free or suitable for a particular patient. An illustration cannot establish that.
- Direct product-specific questions to the representative. Medical advice belongs in reviewed material with sources, outside this page's current scope.
- Recheck source facts whenever the representative, public links, visit arrangements or identity changes.
